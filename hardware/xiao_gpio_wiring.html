EXTERNAL RESISTORS & RULES

Part Value Why It Is Needed
R1 (D1) 10 kΩ
to 3V3		 Pull-up for bare reed switch. Holds D1 at 3.3V (1) when open; reed pulls D1 to 0V (0) when magnet passes. (Included onboard if using KY-021 module.)
R2 (D0) 10–100 kΩ
to GND		 Pull-down for main button. Holds D0 at 0V (0) when idle; button pulls D0 to 3.3V (1) when pressed.
R3 / R4 100 kΩ /
380 kΩ		 Scales 4.2V battery to 3.32V at D2 (AIN4), switched by D3 (P0.29) FET so divider draws 0 µA in sleep.
CRITICAL: Neither D0 (`P0.02`) nor D1 (`P0.03`) enables internal nRF52840 pull resistors in devicetree. Never leave D0 or D1 floating, and never connect 5V to any GPIO pin.
