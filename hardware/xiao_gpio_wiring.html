RESISTORS & MODULE WIRING

Circuit Pull / Value Wiring Notes
Main Btn
(D0 / P0.02)		 Internal
GPIO_PULL_DOWN
(~13 kΩ) 		Enabled in devicetree overlay. Wire button directly between 3V3 and D0 (no external resistor needed). Idle = 0V (0), Pressed = 3.3V (1).
KY-021 Reed
(D1 / P0.03)		 Onboard 10 kΩ
(SMD 103) 		3-pin KY-021 module: S → D1, middle (+) → 3V3, − → GND. Open = 3.3V (1), Closed = 0V (0). (If open reads 0V on your board, swap + and − between 3V3 and GND.)
Battery Sense
(BAT+ / BAT−)		 Onboard XIAO
1 MΩ / 510 kΩ
(P0.31 / P0.14) 		Included on XIAO PCB! Wiring battery to backside BAT+ / BAT− uses XIAO's internal divider (P0.31/AIN7 + P0.14). External D2/D3 circuit is optional (DK only).
CRITICAL: Always power the KY-021 module from the 3V3 pin (never 5V), and never apply 5V to any nRF52840 GPIO pin.
